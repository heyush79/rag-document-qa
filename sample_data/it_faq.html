Acme IT FAQ

How do I reset my password?

Open the self-service portal at reset.acme.example and choose Forgot password. A reset link is emailed to your manager-approved recovery address and expires after 15 minutes.

How do I connect to the VPN?

Install the Acme VPN client from the software center, sign in with your company account and approve the multi-factor prompt. The VPN disconnects automatically after 12 hours.

Who do I contact when my laptop is lost or stolen?

Report it to the security team on the #security channel or by phone within one hour, then open a hardware ticket. Lost devices are wiped remotely the same day.

What is the on-call escalation path?

  • First: the on-call engineer for the service, paged through the alerting tool.
  • Second: the team lead, after 15 minutes without acknowledgement.
  • Third: the engineering manager, after 30 minutes.
