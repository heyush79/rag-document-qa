# Acme Engineering Handbook

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Team members should coordinate their in-office days with their manager. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in the employee's local time zone.

## Paid leave
Full-time employees receive 24 paid leave days per calendar year. Leave requests longer than five consecutive working days should be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Emergency leave can be submitted retrospectively with manager approval.

## Security
Company source code must only be stored in approved repositories. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for source control, cloud accounts, and the corporate VPN. Suspected credential exposure must be reported to the security team within one hour.

## Learning budget
Each employee receives an annual learning budget of INR 40,000. The budget may be used for technical books, courses, certification exams, and approved conferences. Manager approval is required before purchase.
